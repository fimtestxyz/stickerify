Publish your pack on Telegram

  1. Unzip stickerify-telegram-pack.zip — it holds one file per sticker, plus a pack icon.
  2. In Telegram, open @Stickers and send /newpack, then a name for your pack.
  3. Send each sticker file as a document (📎 → File, not Photo) so Telegram doesn't compress it.
  4. Send one emoji for each sticker when the bot asks.
  5. Send /publish, choose a short link name, and share your t.me/addstickers/… link.

Packs hold up to 120 stickers. Your photos never left this device — only the sticker files you just downloaded go to Telegram, when you choose to send them.

How it works

  1. Segment. A MediaPipe multiclass selfie-segmentation model (hair, face, body skin, clothes) finds every head — or the whole person, if you pick Full body — in the photo, on your device. The model downloads once (~16 MB) with a progress bar, then stays cached.
  2. Batch. Drop in as many photos as you like — up to three are segmented at the same time in background workers, each with its own status.
  3. Cut out. Each head (or whole person) is cut out with a soft edge, keeping hair, face and clothing detail.
  4. Stickerify. A thick outline, soft shadow, and optional background disc turn each cut-out into a sticker. Pick a costume to put a head on a cartoon body (Superhero, Astronaut, Bunny, Dino). Download one, or all of them as a sheet.

No accounts, no servers, no tracking. If the multiclass model cannot load, the app falls back to a binary person-segmentation model plus face detection. This tool does not identify anyone — it only finds head and body shapes, and it never stores a photo.
