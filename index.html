How it works

  1. Segment. A MediaPipe multiclass selfie-segmentation model (hair + face) finds every head in the photo, on your device. The model downloads once (~16 MB) with a progress bar, then stays cached.
  2. Batch. Drop in as many photos as you like — up to three are segmented at the same time in background workers, each with its own status.
  3. Cut out. Each head is cut out with a soft edge, keeping hair and face detail.
  4. Stickerify. A thick outline, soft shadow, and optional background disc turn each cut-out into a sticker. Download one, or all of them as a sheet.

No accounts, no servers, no tracking. If the multiclass model cannot load, the app falls back to a binary person-segmentation model plus face detection. This tool does not identify anyone — it only finds head shapes, and it never stores a photo.
