How it works

  1. Segment. A MediaPipe multiclass selfie-segmentation model (hair + face) finds every head in the photo, on your device.
  2. Cut out. Each head is cut out with a soft edge, keeping hair and face detail.
  3. Stickerify. A thick outline, soft shadow, and optional background disc turn each cut-out into a sticker. Download one, or all of them as a sheet.

No accounts, no servers, no tracking. If the multiclass model cannot load, the app falls back to a binary person-segmentation model plus face detection. This tool does not identify anyone — it only finds head shapes, and it never stores a photo.
