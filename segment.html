Publish your pack on Telegram

  1. Unzip stickerify-telegram-pack.zip — it holds one file per sticker, plus a pack icon.
  2. In Telegram, open @Stickers and send /newpack, then a name for your pack.
  3. Send each sticker file as a document (📎 → File, not Photo) so Telegram doesn't compress it.
  4. Send one emoji for each sticker when the bot asks.
  5. Send /publish, choose a short link name, and share your t.me/addstickers/… link.

Packs hold up to 120 stickers. Your photo never left this device — only the sticker files you just downloaded go to Telegram, when you choose to send them.

How it works

  1. Segment. A MediaPipe DeepLabV3 model recognises 21 kinds of object — people, dogs, cats, cars, bottles, chairs and more — in the photo, on your device. The model downloads once (~2.7 MB) with a progress bar, then stays cached.
  2. Background removed. Everything the model recognises stays; the rest goes transparent.
  3. Objects. Each separate blob of the same kind becomes its own sticker. Tap to select the ones you want, then download them all at once as a ZIP — or as a Telegram pack.

Limits: the model knows 21 kinds of object. Things outside that list are treated as background, and two objects of the same kind that touch each other come out as one sticker. If the splitting looks wrong, uncheck "Split into individual objects" above — you still get the background-removed photo. This tool does not identify anyone — it only finds object shapes, and it never stores a photo.
